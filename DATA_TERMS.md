# Data rights, attribution and provenance

The project aims to make factual window and door reference information usable in estimating and BIM. It links back to manufacturer product sources rather than distributing their catalogues.

- This does not grant a licence to third-party catalogue text, protected database content, designs, logos, images, drawings, trademarks, BIM families or software. Manufacturer names and product references identify their respective products. Original manufacturer documents are not included in the repository.
- Source publications may change or be superseded. Source references and retrieval dates are maintained in `data/sources.csv`. Directory listings are historical or supplier leads, not evidence of current domestic manufacture or accreditation.
- Before publicly distributing or commercially reusing derived catalogue information, review any applicable source terms and permission requirements. This repository does not claim blanket third-party permission to redistribute all catalogue content.

No representation is made that every source, extracted record or ordering option is current or correct. Confirm dimensions and performance with the manufacturer for each project.
